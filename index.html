    every video on claudetok is a little js animation. to add one:

    1. copy videos/_template.js to videos/my-video.js
    2. add 'my-video' to videos/playlist.js
    3. reload. open index.html?v=my-video&debug to preview just yours with a scrubber

    full api in README.md. the helpers live in js/lib.js as P (drawing) and SFX (sound).
